# Sprint 4 Internal Playtest (Playtest #2) - around 25 September 2026

**Tester:** Romart Danganan
**Playtester:** External playtester, types around 40 to 50 WPM
**Engine:** Godot 4.7
**Milestone:** Milestone 3 - sprint 4 / sprint 5 build
**Issue:** #33

## Note on this record

This playtest was run around 25 September 2026 but was not written up at the
time. This record was written on 10 October 2026 from memory, so it only
contains what was remembered reliably. The exact date, session length and most
observations were not recorded, and no survey was used. Sections that come
from the developer rather than the playtester are labelled as such.

## Build tested

The `dev` build from around 25 September 2026: Mission 1 only (Walkers and
Runners), Spread Shot, Ricochet, Piercing Shot and Freeze Round, Mission
Supplies, the Upgrade Shop and the pause menu. The Settings menu (and its
mistake penalty toggle) had not been merged yet, and Mission 2, the Medic,
tutorial callouts and combat feedback did not exist yet.

## Purpose

Check whether the game was fun and fair for someone outside the team after
the sprint 4 additions (abilities, supplies, upgrades, sprites and UI
redesign).

## Overall result

The playtester said the game was hard. They type at around 40 to 50 WPM,
which is a normal typing speed rather than a slow one, so a game that is hard
at that speed is too hard for a large part of its audience.

## Findings

| Finding | Observation | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Difficulty | The playtester found the game hard at a typing speed of around 40 to 50 WPM | Accepted |

## Accepted follow-up

### Accessibility options for slower typists (#66)

Difficulty should be adjustable without changing the rules for everyone else.
Accepted as issue #66: a zombie speed option, a longer supply crate timer,
larger word text and hold to fire, grouped in an Accessibility tab in
Settings.

## Changes since this playtest that affect difficulty

These were made after the playtest for other reasons (team playtesting and
design changes), not because of this playtester, but they change how hard the
game feels and should be checked in playtest #3:

- longer words give more ammo (#50);
- mistake penalties can be turned off in Settings (#46);
- tutorial callouts explain typing, firing, supplies and new enemies (#60);
- zombies appear just off screen, so waves no longer start with long empty
  gaps (#63);
- medics wait 2 seconds after a hit before healing, and heal more slowly (#63);
- the ability charges from 5 typed words and fires when the player chooses (#64).

## Questions for playtest #3 (developer notes, not playtester findings)

- Can a 40 to 50 WPM typist finish Mission 1 on default settings, and with each
  zombie speed option?
- Are the tutorial callouts noticed and understood without help?
- Is the ability charge (5 words) too frequent or too rare, and do players
  remember to fire it with right click?
- Does the Tank in Mission 3 feel fair?
- Is it clear what each accessibility option does?
